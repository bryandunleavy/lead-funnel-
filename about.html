About Bryan

Bryan has a natural passion for real estate and an in-depth understanding of the Upstate market. He is proud to be part of eXp Realty, which gives him technology, marketing tools, and training so clients get exceptional service without the old-school brokerage overhead.

His career as a realtor began in 2017. Since then he has helped clients buy and sell across Greenville, Travelers Rest, Simpsonville, Anderson, and surrounding communities. Clients describe him as honest, reliable, and trustworthy — someone who listens, negotiates well, and stays engaged through the entire process.

He is an Upstate native who cares about the growth of this area. Outside of transactions he is active in the community, including leadership with the Greenville St. Patrick’s Day Parade & Festival and the Ancient Order of Hibernians.
